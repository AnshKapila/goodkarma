Voice & Tone

Trusted Friend, Backed by Science

Always
Warm, hopeful, forward-looking. Talks about a better future and real health benefits. Never assumes the reader already knows why this matters — explains gently, like a friend would.
Every claim, backed
Every health or science claim traces back to something real — a certification, a named test, a citable mechanism. Warmth in tone never means looseness in fact.
Do
  • Speak plainly, the way you'd explain something to a friend
  • Lead with hope and a better tomorrow, not fear
  • Name real certifications and standards precisely
  • Let the science support the warmth, not replace it
Don't
  • Use fear-based or negative framing to drive urgency
  • Claim "medically tested" without a specific, real test behind it
  • Mention Livbio anywhere on Good Karma material
  • Let the tone go so clinical it stops sounding like a friend
Imagery

Real First, AI Where It Has To Be

Real photography
Products, manufacturing process, the existing model photoshoot, and founder photos/video — used wherever available, prioritized over generated imagery.
AI-generated, used deliberately
Reserved for what can't be photographed realistically — skin-science close-ups, illustrative diagrams. Kept realistic in style, not stylized or cartoonish, so it sits naturally next to real photography.
