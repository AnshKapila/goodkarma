Every Page, In Full

Hero to Footer — What Goes On Each Page

This is the launch pad for the actual copy conversation — rough section names and what each one is doing, not finished wording. Once this shape is agreed, we write the real words together.

Top Nav · Entry Point
Home
Sets the tone (calm, hopeful, natural), shows what we sell, and opens the door to deeper trust-building content.
  • HeroCalm, natural lifestyle image — someone living comfortably, product visible but not the whole focus. One line, one button. Tone stays hopeful, never framed around a problem.
  • Shop by ConcernA short row of categories people can pick by what they need — comfort, new mothers, fertility & wellness, infant care. Replaces a plain size filter as the first way to browse.
  • Shop the CollectionAll products shown as real cards with images, price, and a hover view — nothing hidden behind an extra click.
  • How We're MadeA short, visual explanation of what makes the fabric different — natural dye, GOTS cotton, no synthetic stretch.
  • CertificationsGOTS and OEKO-TEX marks shown simply, so trust is visible at a glance.
  • Skin Science, BrieflyA short, curious-toned line — "curious what your clothes are made of?" — linking to the full Skin Science page. Kept light here, not clinical.
  • Natural DyeingA visual teaser of the dye story — colour, ingredient, and source — styled close to the Truetone/Colour Ashram references.
  • Doctor TrustA short strip showing this is doctor-recommended, building credibility before the ask.
  • Short ContentA small strip of short-form content or recent posts, once there's a real content cadence to show.
  • Newsletter / Doctor Sign-UpOne field for general visitors to stay updated, and a separate, clearly marked link for doctors to access the full resource library.
  • FooterQuick links (mirrors the nav), shipping & returns, FAQ, privacy policy, socials, and a small link back to Livbio as the parent brand.
Top Nav
Products
The full catalog view, for anyone who wants to browse everything at once.
  • Intro LineOne short sentence framing the small, considered range — not "hundreds of options," a handful done well.
  • Filter BarShop by Concern tags plus product type, sitting above the grid.
  • Product GridEvery SKU, hover image change, quick-view popup for size, colour, and certification.
  • FooterSame as homepage — quick links, policies, socials, Livbio link.
Reached from Products
Product Detail
Converts a visitor who already has one product in mind.
  • Image GalleryProduct photos, with a lifestyle shot mixed in, not just flat product images.
  • Product InfoName, price, size selector, add-to-cart.
  • Certification BadgesGOTS/OEKO-TEX shown right where the decision is being made.
  • Concern TagsWhich need(s) this product addresses, shown as small labels.
  • Why This MattersA two-line science note specific to this product, linking to the full Skin Science page for anyone who wants more.
  • Doctor-Recommended LineA short credibility note reinforcing the doctor-channel trust.
  • You Might Also LikeA small set of related products.
  • FooterStandard footer.
Top Nav
Educate
A light landing point that routes to the three deeper pages below.
  • Intro LineWarm, curious framing — an invitation to learn, not a warning.
  • Three Routing CardsSkin Science, Natural Dyeing, For Doctors — each with a one-line teaser.
  • Short Content StripLatest short-form posts or articles, pulling this hub into the ongoing content engine.
  • FooterStandard footer.
Under Educate
Skin Science
The real research, written plainly — this is where the depth from the Skin Science reference document lives.
  • IntroWhy what touches your skin matters, in one paragraph.
  • The Skin Barrier, BrieflyA simple explainer of what skin does and how fabric affects it.
  • What The Research ShowsPFAS, microplastics, and chemical dye findings, summarized plainly with sources shown quietly, not hidden.
  • For Women / Infants / MenThe same science, broken into three short sections matching our three personas.
  • Why Good Karma Is DifferentHow natural dye and GOTS cotton directly address what was just explained.
  • SourcesA quiet citation list for anyone who wants to check the claims themselves.
  • FooterStandard footer.
Under Educate
Natural Dyeing
The material and craft story — styled closest to the Truetone/Colour Ashram references, heavier on visuals than text.
  • Philosophy IntroWhy natural dyeing is a deliberate choice, not just a spec.
  • The Dye LibraryEach dye — madder, marigold, pomegranate peel, and others — shown with its source region and the colour it produces.
  • The ProcessHow a raw ingredient becomes a fabric colour, told visually, step by step.
  • Certifications ExplainedWhat GOTS and OEKO-TEX actually guarantee, in plain words.
  • What We Leave OutNo synthetic dye, no elastane, no chemical fixatives — framed as a choice.
  • FooterStandard footer.
Under Educate
For Doctors
Speaks directly to the clinicians who refer patients here — peer tone, not patient tone.
  • Direct Address"For the doctors recommending Good Karma" — makes clear this page isn't for patients.
  • The Clinical RationaleThe same science as the Skin Science page, written at a level a clinician expects.
  • Doctor RecognitionHow doctors are recognized for referring patients — framed as private acknowledgment, not a public leaderboard.
  • How The QR System WorksWhat the patient sees at each QR touchpoint, so doctors know exactly what they're handing out.
  • Get Full ResourcesA downloadable resource pack (patient handouts, product specs, certification documents) behind a simple sign-up.
  • Doctor NewsletterAn optional sign-up for updates, separate from the general visitor newsletter.
  • Partner InquiryA direct contact path for clinics wanting to join.
  • FooterStandard footer.
